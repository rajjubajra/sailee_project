Self-taught artist and poet, she begins in stillness—brushes, pigments, and paper scattered around her, yet her mind already in motion. Her creative process is deeply meditative, surfacing images and emotions from the subconscious.

Her work drifts between abstraction and figuration, never fully committing to one, leaving room for the viewer's own contemplation. Each piece reads like a journal entry, an intimate glimpse into her inner landscape. Through her art, she evokes the quiet truth of impermanence—each stroke a moment held, then released.

Beyond painting, she shapes clay into chawans, finding rhythm in the tactile. Poetry threads through her exhibitions, weaving language into visual space. For her, art is not a product but a practice—a way of being present.
