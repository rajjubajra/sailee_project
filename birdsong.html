Birdsong for the confined

Collection 001 · 12.08.2026

This collection explores the delicate tension between confinement and freedom, using avian motifs as a quiet rebellion against stillness. Each piece is a meditation on the spaces we inhabit — both physical and emotional — and the songs we carry within them. The textures, muted tones, and fragmented silhouettes speak to a world that is at once restrained and fiercely alive.

The work draws from archival field recordings, vintage ornithological illustrations, and the subtle geometry of cage bars. It is an invitation to listen closely, to find melody in the mundane, and to remember that even in confinement, there is a way to sing.
